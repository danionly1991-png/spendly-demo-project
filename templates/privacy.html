Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information Spendly collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to your use of the Spendly personal expense tracking service ("Service").

1. Data We Collect

We collect only what we need to run the Service:

  • Account information — your name, email address, and a securely hashed password.
  • Expense data — the amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you choose to enter.
  • Basic technical data — such as browser type and session information needed to keep you signed in.

Spendly does not connect to your bank and never asks for card numbers or bank login details.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Store your expenses and show you summaries and category breakdowns.
  • Keep the Service secure and fix problems.
  • Contact you about important changes to the Service or these policies.

We do not sell your personal data or use your expense data for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in a secured database and protected with reasonable technical measures, including password hashing. No method of storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information from unauthorised access.

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. You can delete individual expenses at any time, and deleting your account removes your personal data from our active systems.

4. Third Party Services

We may rely on a small number of trusted providers, such as hosting services and web fonts, to operate Spendly. These providers only receive the information needed to perform their function. We do not share your expense data with third parties except where required by law.

5. Your Rights

You may access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. If you would like a copy of your data or have a request we cannot handle in the app, contact us.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out through the contact details provided in the app. See also our Terms and Conditions.
